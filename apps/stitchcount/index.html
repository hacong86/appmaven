Keep your place

Pick up where your hands left off.

A scarf on the sofa. A blanket for someone special. Another work in progress. StitchCount keeps the small details close, so getting back to your craft feels simple.

One row at a time

Before the first stitch

Turn a swatch into a plan.

The knitting gauge calculator uses your swatch and target dimensions to estimate stitch and row counts. Check your cast-on plan before starting the full piece.

The yarn yardage calculator helps estimate length and skeins in meters or yards. Needle and crochet hook size references are close by, too.

A small planning example

A 10 cm swatch with 22 stitches and 30 rows gives a starting estimate of 110 stitches and 180 rows for a 50 × 60 cm rectangle.

Allow for borders, stitch repeats and your pattern instructions. Gauge and yarn estimates guide your planning; they cannot guarantee the final fit or yarn use.

Good to know

A few thoughtful details.

Can I use StitchCount for both knitting and crochet?

Yes. Count rows, stitches or rounds, follow repeating sections, and keep separate project notes for either craft.

Does StitchCount work offline?

Core counting, project notes and craft tools work offline. Advertising, consent services and Google Play purchases use an internet connection.

Are patterns included?

Bring your own pattern PDFs. StitchCount organizes the instructions you already have; it does not provide a pattern marketplace or generate patterns.

How do I keep a copy of my projects?

Use manual backup in Settings and keep the exported backup somewhere safe. There is no automatic AppMaven cloud sync. The StitchCount privacy policy explains storage, sharing and deletion.
