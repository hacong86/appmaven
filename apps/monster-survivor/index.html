How It Plays

Monster Survivor: Pet RPG is a survival roguelike. Each run starts simple: move with one finger, dodge the monsters closing in, and collect Soul while your weapons attack on their own.

Every level-up offers a choice of new weapons and upgrades, and the right combinations evolve into stronger forms. Summon monster pets in the middle of battle, capture creatures for your collection, and survive long enough to face the boss of the map.

Between runs, spend gold on permanent upgrades, level up and evolve your pets, unlock new heroes and open new maps. Come back stronger and push your survivor further.

FAQ

How do I control my hero?
Put your thumb anywhere on the screen and a virtual joystick appears under it; drag to move. Your weapons attack automatically, so you can focus on dodging and collecting Soul.
How do monster pets work?
During a run you can summon pets into the arena to fight with you, and capture monsters to add them to your collection. Between runs you can level up, evolve and fuse your pets.
How do I unlock new maps and heroes?
Swipe between maps on the map select screen and unlock new ones with Gold or Gems. Wave is ready from the start; other heroes unlock with Gold or Gems, by capturing enough monster species, or by reaching a higher player level.
Does the game have ads?
Yes. Rewarded videos are optional, for example to revive, reroll upgrade choices or get extra gold. Full-screen ads may appear after some lost runs, and banner ads show in menus. Remove Ads turns off the banner and full-screen ads; rewarded videos stay optional.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your progress. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack are tied to your Google account and can be restored from Google Play; Gems are not restored. See the privacy policy for details.

A Survival Roguelike Where Your Pets Join the Fight

In Monster Survivor: Pet RPG, every run is a short fight for survival. Monsters pour in from every side, and your position matters more than your reflexes on a button. Find the gaps, keep moving and let your weapons thin out the horde.

Every Run Is a New Build

Level-ups pause the action and offer a few choices: a new weapon, an upgrade for one you already have, or a passive bonus. Weapons can be combined and evolved, so the same hero can play very differently from one run to the next.

Pets Matter in Battle

Monster pets are not just collection icons. Summon them during a run and they fight beside your hero. Capture new monsters along the way, then level up, evolve and fuse your favorites between runs.

Bosses and Themed Maps

Each map ends in a boss fight that rewards reading its attacks and dodging at the right time. The sunny Meadow, the foggy Cemetery, the burning Inferno and the frozen Snowfield each bring their own monsters and a higher challenge.

Ads and Purchases

The game is free to play and shows ads. Optional in-app purchases are Gem packs (Gem Pouch, Gem Box, Gem Chest and Gem Vault), Remove Ads and a Starter Pack. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Read more in our Monster Survivor guide.
