Your photos, your choice

Photo Prompt prepares text instructions. It does not generate images, edit photos or upload your pictures. You choose what to paste or attach in an external AI service.

Saved prompts stay in app storage, with possible Android backup. Google services handle advertising, purchases and optional reviews.

From an idea to a usable prompt

Choose a photo idea instead of starting with an empty message. Each template includes a sample image, customizable choices and guidance on suitable input photos and details to check in the result.

How to use Photo Prompt

  1. Browse a category or search for the photo you want to work on.
  2. Open a template, read its photo tips and adjust the available choices.
  3. Preview and copy the prompt, then open the suggested AI tool.
  4. Paste it there and attach your own photo if the template calls for one.

For supported image conversations, choose or write a follow-up correction and copy it into the same conversation. Check faces, text, materials and other important details before keeping a result.

Keep what works

Save favorites, revisit your copy history and write your own prompts. The bundled templates and sample images can be browsed offline, without a Photo Prompt account. AI services, advertising and purchases need internet access.

Prompts for everyday photo ideas

Old photos and photo fixes

Prepare instructions for old photo restoration, blur, grain, glasses glare, lighting, color casts and background changes. Restoration and colorization involve interpretation; compare the result with your original.

Professional portraits

Start with a CV photo, office headshot or speaker portrait, then adjust the available clothing, lighting and framing choices.

Couples, family and pets

Explore ideas for couples, friends, family moments and pet portraits. Photo tips help you choose inputs; likeness and details still depend on the AI tool.

Celebrations and travel

Browse birthdays, graduation, weddings, Diwali, Eid and other occasions, alongside travel scenes, outfits and art styles.

Products and selling

Build prompts for product backgrounds, material close-ups, actual packaging and food photos. Check that edits preserve the real item's condition and features before publishing a listing.

Guidance and corrections

Read what kind of photo to use and what to inspect. Copy a follow-up instruction when a supported AI conversation needs an adjustment.

Before you start

Which AI tools can I use?

Photo-based prompts are designed for image conversations in ChatGPT or Gemini. Midjourney formatting is available for supported templates. Features, availability and results depend on the service you use.

What is free, and what is PRO?

50 templates are available without a purchase; 82 are PRO. Optional one-time purchases unlock the full catalog and remove ads, or remove ads alone. Rewarded ads can temporarily unlock individual PRO templates when available. Photo Prompt contains ads and has no subscription of its own.

Does a purchase include an AI subscription?

No. External AI services have their own accounts, usage limits and possible charges. A Photo Prompt purchase does not pay for those services.

Are the sample results guaranteed?

No. Sample images illustrate possible ideas. Results vary with your photo, instructions and AI model. Check important details, and review the AI service's terms and privacy policy before uploading photos.

Is this an official app from an AI provider?

Photo Prompt is an independent AppMaven app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Google or Midjourney.
