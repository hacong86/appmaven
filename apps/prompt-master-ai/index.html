Photo Prompt: AI Portrait

120 ready-made prompts for the AI tools you already use. Pick a template, fill in a few choices, copy, and paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini with your own photo.

Get it on Google Play

Privacy in one paragraph

The app never sees your photos — it has no camera permission and no image picker, and you attach your picture inside your AI tool of choice. Your favourites, history and your own prompts stay on your phone. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob unless you buy the ad-free upgrade, and there is no account to create.

What it is

Photo Prompt is a catalogue of written prompts, not a photo editor. It gives you the exact sentence to paste into an AI tool so the picture that comes back is the one you wanted — and so the person in it still looks like you.

Twelve groups cover the reasons people actually reach for a photo: rescuing a damaged one, a headshot for work, a family portrait, a festival, a trip, a pet, something to sell. 44 of the 120 templates are free, with at least one free template in every group.

Everything runs offline. The catalogue and all 120 sample images ship inside the app.

How it works

  1. Open the template you want
  2. Fill in the choices — where, what you are wearing, the mood, the shape
  3. Tap Copy, then open ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney
  4. Paste the prompt and attach your photo

Questions people ask

Does the app edit my photos?

No. Photo Prompt writes the words. You copy the finished prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney together with your own photo, and that tool makes the picture. The app has no camera permission and cannot open your gallery.

Do I need to pay for anything?

44 of the 120 templates are free, including one in every group. The rest unlock with a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

Why not just write the prompt myself?

You can. The difference is in the sentence. Every template here follows the published guidance from Google, OpenAI and Midjourney on what to keep unchanged and what may change, so the person in the result is recognisably you rather than a stranger.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every template and all 120 sample images are bundled inside the app. A connection is only needed for ads and purchases.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Any tool that accepts a written prompt and an image. The app is written against the documented behaviour of ChatGPT, Gemini and Midjourney, and offers a separate Midjourney version of prompts that suit it.
