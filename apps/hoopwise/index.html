Know the file before you hoop

You bought a design, unzipped it and found ten files in formats you have never heard of. Which one does your machine read? Will it fit your hoop? What thread do you need?

Hoopwise opens existing machine embroidery files and shows the real design: true stitch count, size in mm and inches, thread colors in stitching order, and a simulator that stitches it on screen. When your machine needs another format, Hoopwise converts the file and tells you before saving what that format cannot keep.

Hoopwise does not turn pictures into embroidery. It works with embroidery files that already exist.

Key Features

Open 12 formats

PES, DST, JEF, EXP, VP3, HUS, PCS, SEW, XXX, PEC, U01 and 10o, from Files, other apps or a ZIP.

Stitch simulator

Watch the design stitch in order, color by color, at four speeds.

Match thread brands

Swap any color, or match the whole design to Isacord, Sulky, Robison-Anton, Brother, Janome and more.

Convert for your machine

Save as PES, DST, EXP, JEF, VP3, PEC, XXX or U01. Every needle point is kept, and you see what changes before saving.

Pick your machine's embroidery area once; every design shows if it fits, fits when rotated, or how much it is too big.

FAQ

Can it turn a picture into an embroidery file?
No. Hoopwise opens embroidery files that already exist. Turning a picture into stitches (digitizing) needs separate digitizing software.
Can it open EMB, ART or JAN files?
No. Those are design files from desktop software, not machine files. Ask the designer for DST or PES, which Hoopwise opens and can convert.
Does converting change my design?
No. Every needle point is kept in order; only the way it is written changes. Before saving, Hoopwise lists what the new format cannot store, such as colors in DST. Always stitch a test on scrap fabric first.
Is it free? Is there a subscription?
Viewing, simulating and changing colors are free. You get 3 free exports, then you can watch a short ad for an extra export (up to 3 a day). Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited exports and no ads. There is no subscription.
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. Your designs stay on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
How do I get the file onto my machine?
Tap Export, choose your machine's format, tap Save to… and pick your USB drive (iPhone and iPad need a USB-C or Lightning adapter). Then plug the drive into your machine. Hoopwise does not send files to machines over Wi-Fi.
