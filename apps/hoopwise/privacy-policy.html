Privacy Policy for Hoopwise: Embroidery Viewer

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes the Hoopwise: Embroidery Viewer app for iPhone and iPad, developed by AppMaven ("we", "us"). The Bundle ID is com.appmaven.hoopwise. It covers version 1.0 of the app.

At a glance

  • The embroidery files you open, the colors you change and your settings stay on your device. We never receive your designs.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not run our own server, analytics or crash-reporting service for the app.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • The app asks for permission to track only after any required consent form, and works the same if you say no.
  • The optional Pro purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Who is responsible

AppMaven is the developer of Hoopwise and is responsible for the information described in this policy that is handled by the app. Google and Apple process information under their own privacy policies when they provide advertising, consent, purchase and rating services. Contact details are at the end of this page.

2. Information stored on your device

When you open an embroidery file (for example PES, DST, JEF, EXP or VP3), the app copies it into its private storage on your device so it can show it in your Library. The app also stores:

  • the design details it reads from each file (stitches, colors, size), preview images, names, favorites and notes you add;
  • thread colors you change or match to a thread brand;
  • your settings, such as units, your machine's format, embroidery area and appearance;
  • the number of free exports you have used and your Pro status.

Files you import from a ZIP archive are unpacked on your device; temporary files are removed when the import finishes. Converted files and PDF worksheets are created on your device and saved or shared only where you choose in the system Save or Share sheet. The app never changes or deletes your original files outside the app.

None of this information is sent to AppMaven. It is not used for advertising.

3. iCloud and device backups

The app's storage is part of your device. Depending on your settings, iOS may include it in your iCloud Backup or in a backup on your computer. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you; AppMaven cannot access them. Removing the app deletes its local data, but an existing backup may still contain it until the backup is replaced or deleted.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not have Pro, the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a native ad in the Library and on the export finished screen, a full-screen ad that may appear when you leave a design (not in your first sessions, and at most once per session), and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for one extra export. No ads are shown during your first session, and Pro removes all ads.

5. Your advertising choices

Consent form (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states)

Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. The form uses your approximate region and records your choices on your device. When the option is available for your region, you can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app. After a change, ads that were already loaded are discarded and new ones follow your new choice.

Permission to track (App Tracking Transparency)

After the consent form, if one is shown, iOS asks whether Hoopwise may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google can use the device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you ask the app not to track, you still get every feature, rewarded videos still give you exports, and ads are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app does not ask again with its own pop-up.

Age signals in regions that require them

On iOS 26.2 and later, in places where the law requires apps to consider a user's age, the app may ask iOS to share your age range through Apple's Declared Age Range feature. You choose whether to share it. The answer is used only on your device to choose how ads are served: if iOS reports that you are under 18, or if no age range is shared there, the app does not ask for tracking permission and requests only non-personalized ads suitable for all audiences. Only that ad setting is passed to Google with ad requests. Your age range is not stored and is not sent to us.

Pro removes all ads. Choosing Pro, or declining consent or tracking, does not delete data Google has already processed; Google's own controls apply to that data.

6. Purchases through Apple

Hoopwise offers one optional one-time purchase, Pro (lifetime), which unlocks unlimited exports and PDF worksheets and removes ads. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app receives the result of the transaction from Apple to unlock Pro and to restore it with Restore purchases, and keeps your Pro status on your device. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details, payment card number or billing address.

7. Ratings, support emails and links

  • Ratings: when you tap Rate this app, the app shows Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple handles any rating or review you choose to leave.
  • Support emails: Contact support and Report an ad problem open your Mail app with our address and a subject that includes the app version. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. If you do, we receive your email address and what you choose to write or attach, and we use it only to reply and handle your request.
  • Links: links to this policy and to the Terms open in your browser, outside the app.

8. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, photos, microphone, contacts or location. You choose files to open with the system file picker, and the app only reads the files you pick. The only permission the app may request is permission to track for advertising (section 5).

9. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your designs or any information about you, and we do not receive your designs. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled as described in sections 4 and 5. Under some US state laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, using device identifiers for personalized advertising may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out by asking the app not to track (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) and, where offered, through Privacy options in the app.

11. Your privacy rights and legal bases

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Because your designs never leave your device, most requests about them can be handled directly in the app.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, advertising that requires consent relies on your consent; providing Pro relies on performing our contract with you; answering support emails relies on your request; and security and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests and legal obligations.

Google and Apple may process information outside your country. Their policies describe the safeguards they use, such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Children

Hoopwise is a tool for machine embroidery and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

iOS keeps the app's storage separate from other apps. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No device, storage system or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change, for example when an Android version is released, and change the date at the top. Review the policy that applies to the version you use.
