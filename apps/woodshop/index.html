Six tools, one bench

The shop math you do every week.

From the lumber yard to the last cut, WoodShop Toolkit keeps the numbers in one place. Projects and calculations are stored on your phone — no account, no subscription. New to the app? Read the step-by-step guide.

01 / BOARD FEET

Board foot calculator

Enter nominal or measured sizes, quarter thicknesses like 4/4 and 8/4, and several boards in one tally. Price by the board foot, per piece or per cubic meter and see material cost, an extra purchase allowance and your budget.

02 / FRACTIONS

Feet & inch calculator

Add, subtract, multiply and divide measurements like 3′ 2½″ on a keypad built for fractions. Round to 1/16, 1/32 or 1/64 inch and see millimeters alongside.

03 / SPACING

Even spacing

Lay out balusters, pickets, slats or shelves with equal gaps or on-center spacing. A diagram and a position table you can copy show where every piece goes.

04 / CUT LIST

Cut list optimizer

Plan plywood and sheet goods in 2D and boards in 1D with grain direction, saw kerf, edge trim and the stock you actually have. Use Fast search, or let Deep search try more layouts.

05 / REFERENCE

Lumber sizes

Look up nominal and actual lumber dimensions, hardwood quarter thicknesses, plywood and MDF sizes, and convert units.

06 / KEEP

Projects & PDFs

Save projects, import or paste CSV parts, share project files, make a local backup and print cut diagrams or tallies as Letter or A4 PDFs.

Before the first cut

See every sheet before you buy it.

Add your parts and the stock you plan to use. WoodShop Toolkit lays the parts out on each sheet or board, shows the cut order, lists parts that do not fit, marks reusable offcuts and builds a shopping list of the stock it used.

Change a size or the saw kerf and the layout is recalculated on your phone. When the plan looks right, export it as a PDF to take into the shop.

A small example

A bookshelf with two 72″ sides, four 34½″ shelves, a 36″ top and two kick boards fits on two 4×8 sheets of birch plywood with 15 cuts, placing 9 of 9 pieces, with a 1/8″ kerf.

Results depend on your inputs. Always measure your actual stock and check the plan before cutting; diagrams are scaled guides, not full-size templates.

Good to know

Questions woodworkers ask.

Does WoodShop Toolkit work offline?

Yes. Calculations, cut-list optimization and PDF creation run on your phone. Ads, the consent form and Google Play purchases use an internet connection.

Can I use metric measurements?

Yes. Work in inches or millimeters, price lumber per cubic meter, and see metric values next to fraction results.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no subscription. Your projects stay on your device; use Settings → Data to create a backup file before changing phones.

Is the cutting diagram a full-size template?

No. Diagrams are scaled guides printed with a calibration line. Always measure your actual stock and verify the plan before cutting.

What data does the app collect?

Your projects are stored only on your device. The free version uses Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers and usage data for ads. The WoodShop Toolkit privacy policy explains what is collected and how to delete it.
