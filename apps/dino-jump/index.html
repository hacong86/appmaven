How It Plays

The volcano behind you has erupted, and the only way out is forward. In Dino Jump: Volcano Escape you guide Pip the little dino through a steaming jungle and a red rock canyon while the ash cloud closes in.

Tap for a short hop, or hold to jump higher and farther. Clear low rocks, tall stumps, lava pits and rolling boulders, dodge swooping pterosaurs and falling meteors, and grab every piece of shining amber on the way.

Stumble on a rock and the volcano gains on you; run clean to pull ahead. Spend your amber to hatch new dinos and buy shields, then try to beat your record distance.

FAQ

How do I play?
Pip runs on its own. Tap anywhere for a short hop, or hold to jump higher and farther. Time your jumps to clear rocks, stumps, lava pits and boulders, and land on running lizards for a bonus bounce.
How do I unlock Rocco and Shelly?
Collect amber during your runs, then open the collection and hatch their eggs: Rocco costs 600 amber and Shelly costs 1,000 amber. Rocco can double jump in mid-air, and Shelly starts every run with a free shield.
What do shields do?
A shield blocks one hit, so a single mistake does not end your run. You can buy shields with amber in the shop or get 3 with the optional Shield Pack purchase.
Does the game have ads?
Yes. Rewarded ads are optional, for example to revive once after a fall or to double your amber. Full-screen ads may appear between some runs, never during a run. There are no banner ads. Remove Ads turns off the ads between runs; rewarded ads stay optional.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your progress. Remove Ads is tied to your Google account and can be restored from Google Play; Shield Pack shields are not restored. See the privacy policy for details.

A One-Tap Dino Runner With a Volcano on Your Tail

Dino Jump: Volcano Escape is an endless arcade runner built around a single touch. Every run starts with the eruption: Pip bolts away from the volcano and the chase is on. The longer you hold, the higher Pip jumps, so every obstacle is a small decision between a quick hop and a long, floating leap.

Every jump is fair

Every stretch of track is checked so it can always be cleared at that speed. Obstacles appear with enough time to react, and meteors show a warning before they land. When you fall, it is on you, and the next run is one tap away.

Short runs, long goals

A run fits a coffee break and plays with one hand in portrait. Between runs, your amber goes toward hatching eggs and buying shields, and your record distance is always there to beat. Grab a full arc of amber for a Perfect! and clear a group of obstacles without a stumble for a Clean!

Ads and purchases

The game is free to download. It shows ads between some runs, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to revive or to double your amber. Optional in-app purchases are Remove Ads, which turns off the ads between runs, and a Shield Pack with 3 shields. Questions or feedback: support@appmavenstudio.com.
