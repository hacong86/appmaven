Privacy Policy for Dino Jump: Volcano Escape

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy describes the Dino Jump: Volcano Escape Android game (the "Game"), developed by AppMaven ("we", "us"). The package name is com.appmaven.dinojump. It covers version 1.0 of the Game.

At a glance

  • There is no account or sign-in. Your progress, amber, dinos, shields and settings are saved on your device. We do not run a server that stores your game data.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes device identifiers, approximate location from your IP address, app interactions and diagnostics to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • Optional purchases are handled by Google Play through Unity IAP. We never see your card number or billing address.
  • Where the law requires it, Google's consent message asks for your choice before ads load. You can change it later under Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves its own data in app storage on your device: your amber balance, record distance, the dinos you have hatched and the one you selected, shields in stock, the date of your last daily gift, tutorial and intro progress, your settings (music, sound effects and haptics) and a cached copy of your Remove Ads purchase status. This data stays on the device. The Game itself does not send it to AppMaven or to anyone else.

Because there is no account or cloud save, uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage removes this data from the device.

2. Android backup and device transfer

The Game does not opt out of Android's default backup. Depending on your Android version, device and backup settings, the Game's saved data may be included in a backup linked to your Google account or copied when you move to a new device. These backups are managed by Android and Google under your settings; AppMaven cannot access or read them. You can turn backup off in your device settings. Removing the Game does not necessarily delete an existing backup.

3. Advertising and consent

The Game uses Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to show:

  • Rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to revive once after a fall or to double the amber you collected or received as a daily gift.
  • Full-screen ads between some runs, shown after a run has ended and never while you are playing.

The Game does not show banner ads. To provide ads, Google's SDK collects and shares the following data with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, security and compliance:

  • Approximate location: your IP address may be used to estimate your general area. The Game does not request precise location.
  • App interactions: events such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics: information about how the app and the advertising SDK perform.
  • Device or other identifiers: the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other identifiers described by Google.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for and record your advertising privacy choices where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and to offer an opt-out where US state privacy laws provide one. Ads are requested only when UMP allows it. Your choice is stored on the device. When it applies in your region, open Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS in the Game to review or change it. Declining personalized ads does not stop all ads; you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (the name of the setting varies by device). These controls affect future processing; they do not by themselves delete data Google has already processed.

The Remove Ads purchase turns off the full-screen ads between runs. Rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch them, and the advertising SDK remains part of the Game.

4. Google Play purchases

The Game offers two optional purchases: Remove Ads (a one-time purchase) and a Shield Pack with 3 shields (a consumable purchase). Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's policies. We do not receive your payment card number or billing address.

Purchases are handled by Unity IAP, a component from Unity Technologies, together with the Google Play Billing Library. The Game receives the product, the purchase status, the order details and the purchase token from Google Play. It checks the purchase receipt on your device, delivers the item, confirms the purchase with Google Play and restores Remove Ads when you reinstall or tap RESTORE PURCHASES.

According to Unity's data disclosure for Unity IAP 5.4 and later, Unity IAP collects approximate location, user identifiers, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostics, other app performance data and device identifiers, for app functionality and analytics, and does not share them with third parties. See Unity IAP's Google Play data safety information and the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your Google Play purchase history is kept by Google. Clearing the Game's storage is not a refund and does not remove purchase records.

5. Ratings and reviews

The Game includes Google's Play In-App Review library. Version 1.0 does not open the in-app rating dialog. If a later version offers it, rating the Game will always be optional. Any rating or review you leave on Google Play, inside the Game or on the store page, is collected and may be published by Google Play under Google's policies; the Game does not receive its contents. You can manage or delete your Google Play reviews at any time.

6. Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to answer you and handle your request. Please do not send payment card details.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • The Game has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own beyond the Google and Unity components described above.
  • The Game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

8. Permissions and security

The Game and its Google components use these Android permissions: internet and network state access (to load ads and process purchases), the advertising ID and Android ad services permissions (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and measurement), Google Play Billing (for purchases), vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the Game's Settings), and wake lock and foreground service (used by Google libraries to finish background tasks). The Game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photo library or location permissions.

Android protects the Game's local storage inside its app sandbox. No system or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Retention and deletion

There is no Dino Jump account to delete, and we do not hold your game data on a server. We cannot delete records kept by Google or Unity on your behalf; use their privacy controls or contact them. For information you have sent us, contact us below. You can also read our general data deletion page.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, personalized advertising relies on your consent; delivering purchases relies on performing our agreement with you; security, fraud prevention and record keeping rely on legitimate interests or legal obligations.

California and other US states: we do not sell personal information for money. However, the use of advertising identifiers for personalized ads may be considered "sharing" under the CPRA and similar laws. You can opt out through Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS where it is offered, or by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your Android settings. You also have the right to know, to delete and not to be discriminated against for using your rights. Contact us to make a request.

11. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International processing

Google and Unity may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe where they process data and the safeguards they use, such as standard contractual clauses.
