Build your own sleep sound

Some nights you want soft rain on a window. Other nights a steady fan or brown noise works better. Rain & Ocean Sleep Sounds lets you layer the sounds you like, set each volume, and keep the mix playing while the screen is off.

Start from a ready-made scene such as Bioluminescent Lagoon or Rainy Roof Cabin, or tap sounds one by one. When you are ready to sleep, set a timer and the sound fades out slowly instead of stopping suddenly.

FAQ

What sounds are included?
63 sounds in 9 groups: rain and thunder, ocean and water, white, pink, brown and green noise, fan and cabin hums, nature and wind, cozy places such as a fireplace or coffee shop, and meditation bells.
Does the sound keep playing when the screen is off?
Yes. Your mix keeps playing in the background, and you can pause or resume it from the notification.
How does the sleep timer work?
Choose 15 minutes to 2 hours. When the time is up, the sound fades out over the fade-out length you set instead of stopping all at once.
How many sounds can I play at once?
Up to 4 sounds at the same time, or 8 with Premium. Each sound has its own volume.
Does the app have ads, and what does Premium include?
The app shows ads. Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play that removes ads, unlocks every sound, scene and breathing pattern, lets you mix 8 sounds, save unlimited favorites and see full sleep insights.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in. Your favorite mixes, sleep journal and settings are stored on your device. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Rain sounds, ocean waves and white noise in one sleep sound mixer

Different people fall asleep to different sounds. Rain & Ocean Sleep Sounds puts the most requested ones in one place and lets you combine them, so you do not have to switch between separate rain, ocean and white noise apps.

Rain sounds for sleep

Choose soft rain for a light background, heavy rain for a fuller sound, or rain on a window, a tent or leaves for a sense of place. Add gentle or distant thunder when you want thunderstorm sounds, and lower its volume so it stays in the background.

Ocean sleep sounds

Calm ocean waves, a distant seashore, deep ocean and underwater drift give a slow, rolling rhythm. Ocean waves layered with light rain is one of the ready-made scenes, and you can save your own version as a favorite mix.

White noise, pink noise and brown noise

Steady noise can cover sudden sounds from the street or the next room. White noise is the brightest, pink noise is softer, and brown noise is deeper and rumbling. Green noise, fan noise, an air conditioner and airplane or train cabin hums are also included.

A sleep timer that fades out

Set the timer from 15 minutes to 2 hours. At the end, the sound fades out over the length you choose, so the room goes quiet slowly instead of stopping abruptly.

Background sound for focus

The same mixer works during the day. Pink or brown noise, a coffee shop, a quiet library or soft keyboard sounds can make reading, work and study time feel less distracting.

Rain & Ocean Sleep Sounds is made for relaxation. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Available for Android on Google Play.
