How It Plays

Dino Drop Master: Egg Merge is a relaxing drop and merge physics puzzle about dinosaur eggs. Slide your finger to aim, let go to drop, and watch the eggs roll, bump and settle.

When three matching eggs touch, they glow, crack open and a baby dino hops into your nursery. Every drop lowers the thorny vine a little, and every natural hatch pushes it back up. Keep the pile below the vine and see how far your run can go.

Three babies of one species grow into a young dino, and three young dinos grow into an adult. Your first adult of each species unlocks a new one.

FAQ

How do eggs hatch?
Three eggs of the same species have to touch each other on the board. They light up, crack open and one baby dinosaur hatches. Eggs of different species never hatch together.
What is Hatch Spark?
Spark lets you pick any one egg on the board and hatch it straight away, without a matching set. You earn Spark charges as you hatch eggs during play, and you can also get extra Sparks from an optional reward video or a Spark pack.
How do I unlock new species?
Four species are open from the start. Each time you raise your first adult of a species, you earn a mark, and marks unlock Ankylosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Spinosaurus and Tyrannosaurus in turn. With all eight unlocked, you can start an eight-species Challenge run.
Does the game have ads?
Yes. Some ads are optional reward videos you choose to watch, for example to continue a run once or to get an extra Spark. Ads may also appear between runs. Remove Ads turns off the ads between runs; reward videos stay optional.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. There is no account, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your progress. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack are tied to your Google account and come back from Google Play after a reinstall. See the privacy policy for details.

A Dinosaur Egg Merge Puzzle With Real Physics

Most merge games ask you to slide tiles on a grid. In Dino Drop Master: Egg Merge, every egg is a physical object. Eggs roll into gaps, rest on top of each other and nudge their neighbors, so where you drop matters as much as what you drop. A good drop can set up a hatch two or three moves later.

Hatching Is the Reward

A hatch is more than a score popup. The three eggs glow, crack and a baby dinosaur leaps out and flies to its nest in your nursery. Hatches also push the thorny vine back up, which gives you room to keep going.

Grow, Collect and Unlock

Your nursery carries over between runs. Babies become young dinos, young dinos become adults, and each first adult unlocks another species until all eight are yours: Compsognathus, Velociraptor, Stegosaurus, Triceratops, Ankylosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Spinosaurus and Tyrannosaurus.

Play at Your Own Pace

There are no energy bars or waiting timers, and no fighting. The vine moves with your drops, not with the clock, so you can take your time to plan. Pause at any time and continue the same run later. A short Practice round shows the basics without touching your saved progress.

Ads and Purchases

The game is free to play and shows ads. Optional in-app purchases are Remove Ads, a 5 Spark pack and a Starter Pack (Remove Ads plus 10 Sparks). Purchases are handled by Google Play.
