How It Plays

Square Race is a physics battle you watch and cheer for. Choose an arena, pick a team color and press play. Eight or thirty-two squares burst out from the edges, grab weapons and power-ups, bounce off bumpers and dodge traps.

After a short while the arena edge turns red and starts closing in, pushing every team toward the middle. The last color standing takes the crown, usually after about a minute. Speed it up to 2× or 4×, pause to look closer, or tap a team to highlight its squares.

FAQ

Do I control the squares?
No. Square Race is a battle you watch. You pick the team you cheer for, set the speed and pause when you like; physics, weapons and the closing arena decide who wins.
Is it free?
Yes, the game is free to download and the first 10 arenas are always open. Other arenas and some Workshop items can be unlocked by watching an optional rewarded ad, or all at once with the one-time Premium Pack, which also removes the ads shown between races.
When do ads appear?
Never during a match. A rewarded ad plays only when you tap Watch to unlock something. A full-screen ad may appear when you leave the results screen, at most once every 3 minutes and 3 matches, and not in your first 3 matches of a session.
Does it work offline?
Matches in arenas you can already play and the Workshop editor run on your device without internet. Watching an ad or buying Premium needs a connection.
Can I share the arenas I build?
Yes. The Workshop can save an arena to a file you choose and open arena files from your device, so you can send them to friends through any app you like.

A Square Battle Royale You Watch and Cheer For

Square Race turns simple colored squares into a full battle arena. You do not steer anyone. Instead you choose a side, start the match and enjoy the chaos: squares rush for the nearest weapon, ricochet off bumpers, grab shields and magnets and slip past traps while the safe zone shrinks. Every match plays out differently, so cheering for your color stays exciting from the countdown to the crown.

Weapons, Items and Special Arena Objects

Fourteen weapons, from the basic Blaster and Throwing Knife to the Hammer, Gatling and Sweep Laser, mix with twelve support items such as Heal, Shield, Boost, Magnet, Clone and Decoy. Some arenas add special objects like speed pads, one-way doors, wind zones and gates held open by switches, which give each map its own character.

Build Your Own Arena in the Workshop

The Workshop is a level editor made for touch screens. Drag walls into place, add traps, weapons and items, set spawn points, then press Test to watch a match in your creation right away. Save it on your device or export it as a file to share. Fans of marble race videos and battle simulator sandboxes will feel at home.

Made for Phones and Tablets

The game runs in landscape on Android phones and tablets, with a Reduced Motion setting, separate music and sound controls, and no account or sign-in. Read how the game handles data in the Square Race Privacy Policy.
