Data Deletion Request

We respect your right to privacy. Use this form to request deletion of your personal data from our apps.

Important Information

By submitting this request, you confirm that:

  • You are the owner of the account/data in question
  • You understand this action is irreversible
  • AppMaven Studio will process your request within 30 days
  • Some data may be retained for legal or compliance purposes (anonymized)
  • Account deletion may result in loss of access to app features and purchases

For immediate concerns or questions, contact: support@appmavenstudio.com

Frequently Asked Questions

We typically process data deletion requests within 30 days of submission. We may contact you to verify ownership or clarify which app and data your request covers. We will email you when the request has been processed.

Submitting the form does not immediately delete your data. If you need to follow up, email support@appmavenstudio.com with the app name and the date you submitted your request.

This depends on the app and the data we hold. Where applicable, a full deletion request covers personal data we can identify as yours, such as:

  • Account information, such as your email, username and profile, if the app has an account
  • Content you created and stored on services we control
  • App preferences and activity records linked to your account
  • Analytics records that we hold and can link to you

For an app that stores data only on your device, we cannot erase it remotely. Use the app's delete or reset feature, where available, and manage any backups separately. Check the app's privacy policy for its specific data practices.

Data we are required to retain by law may be excluded; we will explain any applicable exception. Statistics that are already anonymous and cannot identify you may be retained.

Once deletion is completed, we cannot restore the deleted account or data. If you change your mind while a request is pending, contact support@appmavenstudio.com as soon as possible. We cannot guarantee that processing can be stopped.

You may be able to use the app again or create a new account where supported, but your deleted data will not return. Save anything you want to keep before requesting deletion.

A data deletion request does not automatically cancel subscriptions or issue a refund. Manage subscriptions through the store where you purchased them: Google Play or Apple App Store.

Deleting an app account may affect access to premium features. Whether a purchase can be restored depends on the app, the purchase type and your store account. Contact us before deletion if you are unsure. Refund eligibility is determined separately under the relevant store's policy.

Select Other / Multiple Apps and list each app in the Additional Information field. For each app, tell us whether you want all data deleted or only specific data, and include the account email or user ID if it differs.

You can also submit a separate request for each app, or email support@appmavenstudio.com with the same details. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.
