The studio

What we build

AppMaven Studio is an independent Android developer based in Vietnam. We publish focused apps and casual games on Google Play, each built around one clear job. Some are made for Vietnamese users, others for an international audience.

Practical tools

Calculators, scanners and reference apps for specific tasks, such as WoodShop Toolkit for lumber math, ChemLab for chemistry and Smart Scanner for documents.

Planning and daily life

Apps that keep work and routines in order: FocusOne for daily focus, Invoice Studio for invoices and StitchCount for knitting and crochet rows.

Casual games

Puzzle, arcade and survival games for Android, including Mewside, Monster Survivor, Dino Jump and Pictura.
